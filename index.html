What Washington State's Data Actually Tells Us
WA State in the 2026 National Picture
This analysis covers 270K+ WA EV registrations from 1999–2026 — here's where the state stands and what the numbers reveal
A clear slowdown from 2023 peak
49K → 36K → 12K
Registrations peaked in 2023 (58,879) and have since declined — consistent with the national pullback following federal incentive uncertainty
WA holds its own on incentives
Tax Exemption
Washington maintained its EV sales tax exemption — one reason the state is better insulated from national incentive shifts than most
Infrastructure is the real driver
King County
The data shows high-adoption counties have lower average electric range — charging infrastructure, not battery specs, is what drives EV growth in WA
